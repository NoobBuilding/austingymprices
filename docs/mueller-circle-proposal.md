# Mueller circle — proposal, and the recommendation is DON'T

Asked: propose a re-drawn Mueller circle from geography, not from candidate density, and
say what would change. **Nothing has been moved. This is for your approval.**

## Recommendation: keep the circle as it is

The hypothesis was that Mueller looks thin because its circle is drawn too tightly. The
evidence does not support it. **Mueller is small because Mueller is small**, and it was
enumerated properly.

## What the geography actually says

The Mueller development is the redeveloped Robert Mueller airport site, bounded roughly by
Airport Blvd, 51st, Berkman and Manor/Aldrich. Its centroid is **30.2990, -97.7005**.

The current circle centre is **30.29891, -97.70435** — **400 m away**. The centre is
already right; only radius was ever in question.

Distances from that centroid to the natural catchment you named:

| Landmark | Distance |
|---|---|
| Airport Blvd @ 45th | 1,432 m |
| Manor Rd @ Airport | 1,437 m |
| Windsor Park edge (Berkman/51st) | 1,555 m |
| Highland (I-35/Airport) | 2,767 m |

**The current 2,500 m radius already contains all three of the named catchment edges**, with
900 m to spare. The circle is not failing to reach Mueller's natural boundary — it reaches
it comfortably and stops, which is what a Mueller circle should do.

## It was not under-enumerated either

The sweep cache holds 359 businesses with coordinates. **27 of them fall inside the current
Mueller circle.** Mueller was swept, and swept at the same density as everywhere else. Of
those 27, three are listable and one is priced.

That is not a mapping artifact. **That is the answer.**

## What enlargement would cost

| Radius | Enumerated places | Listed pins annexed from other regions |
|---|---|---|
| 2,500 m (current) | 27 | 2 |
| 3,000 m | 37 | 3 |
| **3,500 m** | 44 | **6 — including `hyde-park-gym`** |

**A 3,500 m Mueller contains Hyde Park Gym.** You cannot draw a Mueller that swallows the
gym Hyde Park is named after. That single fact settles it: the radius that would
meaningfully raise Mueller's candidate count is a radius that has stopped describing
Mueller.

Even 3,000 m annexes ATX Tactics and 24 Hour Fitness Hancock Center — both of which
geocoded to Hyde Park on their own addresses, and Hancock Center is a Hyde Park landmark.

Note that **two pins are already inside the current circle while assigned elsewhere**
(`fitcidence` → east-austin, `24-hour-fitness-hancock` → hyde-park). That is the overlapping-
circles problem, not a sizing problem, and it is worth a separate look — but shrinking or
growing Mueller does not fix it.

## Fresh enumeration pass?

**Not needed.** No proposed radius covers meaningful new ground that was never swept: the
27-place figure at 2,500 m and 44 at 3,500 m come from the SAME existing sweep, so the
extra 17 are businesses we already hold and already triaged. Enlarging the circle would
re-bin known businesses, not discover unknown ones.

## What this means for the ledger

Mueller reads **1/3 priced**, and the honest reading is that Mueller genuinely has very few
listable gyms — it is a residential development with a handful of studios, not a fitness
district. Growth there comes from the chain siblings (`docs/chain-census.md`) if any land
inside it, not from redrawing the boundary.

If you want Mueller to look less thin, the options are to list more of what is actually
there, or to stop treating it as a peer region of Downtown and East Austin. **Redrawing the
circle to capture inventory would be sizing the map to flatter the data**, which is the
opposite of what the site is for.
