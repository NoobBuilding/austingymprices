The two chain-wide sources (24 Hour Fitness, YMCA) are accepted on the **YMCA precedent**:
the brand publishes one statement covering every member, and uniformity is *sourced* here
rather than assumed. Per-club variation would have to be published to override it.

Melt Well is the one judgement call in the set: a "vitamin C shower" is a filtered shower
you actually stand in, listed among the studio's own amenities. Reversible in one line if
the owner reads it as equipment rather than a washroom.
